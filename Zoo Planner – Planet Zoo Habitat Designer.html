Zoo Planner

Habitat ideas, outlines, floor plans and decor for the Planet Zoo base game animals (72+ species, no DLC or Planet Zoo 2). Numbers are approximate planning values (m²) — double-check the in-game Zoopedia before building.

Outline is schematic: blue = water, tan = land, green dots = plants, boxes = den/feeder/shade.

Pick several animals. They are sorted into zoo sections, laid out along winding trails with loops, and given water treatment, power and staff areas.

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move around
